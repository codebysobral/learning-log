# Day 036

## Topics Studied
- The `<form>` tag and the `action` and `method` attributes
- The `<label>` element and explicit (`for`/`id`) and implicit association with `<input>`
- `id`, `name`, and `placeholder` attributes in inputs
- The difference between `placeholder` and `label` for accessibility

## What I Learned
I learned how forms are structured in HTML and how the `action` and `method` attributes define form behavior. I understood the importance of associating labels with inputs, both explicitly and implicitly, to improve usability and accessibility. I also learned the purpose of `id`, `name`, and `placeholder`, and why labels should not be replaced by placeholders.

## Practice
- Created a basic form with two text fields
- Tested accessibility by clicking labels to focus inputs
- Compared explicit and implicit label/input associations

## Challenges
- Understanding when to use `id` versus `name`
- Remembering the correct syntax for explicit label association (`for` and `id`)

## Notes
- Labels improve accessibility and user experience.
- Placeholders are helpful hints, but they should not replace labels.
- Explicit label association is generally preferred for clarity and maintainability.
